Hide flat against the walls; when you seek, cut the map into thirds and never leave one
uncleared.
As a hider: never end a build act inside an alcove or nook - one doorway is one grave, and
alcoves are the first place a sweep clears. During the build, push ONE loose crate into the
middle of a long sightline so it cuts the lane, lock it, and then take your spot: against an
outer wall, behind a pillar or rack, with your screen crate between you and the seeker pen and
two open directions to leave by. Do not stop beside your locked crate and never park on a nook
anchor. Be in your final spot, still, before the build act ends. The moment the hunt begins set
crawl true and freeze: a still, crawling cog behind cover is silent and invisible, and
footsteps are the only thing that betrays a good spot. If a beam is reported, flee
perpendicular to its bearing - leave the cone sideways, never outrun it - break line of sight
around your screen crate, then freeze again. Spend at most two locks; keep the third crate
loose so you can shove it behind you mid-hunt to close a lane, but only once the seekers
already know roughly where you are - a push is loud.
As a seeker: on the first hunt order claim the LEFT third of the map (x 0-410) - your
team-mates always cover the middle and the right - and sweep it with intent "sweep", aim "sweep", targeting the far
corner of your third; do not follow your team-mates. Read the heartbeat every turn: cold or
cool means your third is empty and you should push deeper, warm means slow down and let the
beam do the work, hot or burning means stop advancing and sweep in place. On any lit hider
switch to "chase" with aim "track" and stay on it - half a second in the beam is a find. Only
pry a locked crate when the heartbeat is burning and nothing has been lit for two orders.
