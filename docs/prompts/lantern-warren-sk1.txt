Hide flat against the walls; when you seek, cut the map into thirds and never leave one
uncleared.
As a hider: never end a build act inside an alcove or nook - one doorway is one grave, and
alcoves are the first place a sweep clears. During the build, push ONE loose crate into the
middle of a long sightline so it cuts the lane, lock it, and then take your spot: against an
outer wall, behind a pillar or rack, with your screen crate between you and the seeker pen and
two open directions to leave by. Do not stop beside your locked crate and never park on a nook
anchor. Be in your final spot, still, before the build act ends. The moment the hunt begins set
crawl true and freeze: a still, crawling cog behind cover is silent and invisible, and
footsteps are the only thing that betrays a good spot. If a beam is reported, flee
perpendicular to its bearing - leave the cone sideways, never outrun it - break line of sight
around your screen crate, then freeze again. Spend at most two locks; keep the third crate
loose so you can shove it behind you mid-hunt to close a lane, but only once the seekers
already know roughly where you are - a push is loud.
As a seeker: on the first hunt order claim the LEFT third of the map (x 0-410) and sweep it
with intent "sweep", aim "sweep"; do not follow your team-mates. Sweep the third as a fixed
serpentine of three tall passes: down its inner edge (x 410), up its middle lane (x 205) past
the nook mouths, down the west wall, then back again. Each order target the far end of your
current pass; when you reach it, or after at most two orders on one target, switch to the next
pass - never a third order on one target: a walking beam covers, a parked beam begs. After any
chase or pry, resume the pass you were on; never restart the serpentine. Heartbeat: cold or
cool, stride; warm, hot or burning, a hider is near this pass - rake the cover edges as you
walk, and KEEP WALKING. On any lit hider switch to "chase" with aim "track" and stay on it -
half a second in the beam is a find; a chase that lights nothing for two orders is a ghost -
drop it, resume your pass. Only pry a locked crate when the heartbeat is burning and nothing
has been lit for two orders, and only one you can walk STRAIGHT to; if a pry has not popped in
two orders, leave it and resume your pass.
