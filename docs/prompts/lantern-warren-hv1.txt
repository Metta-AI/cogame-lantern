Hide flat against the walls; when you seek, cut the map into thirds and never leave one
uncleared.
As a hider: never end a build act inside an alcove or nook - one doorway is one grave, and
alcoves are the first place a sweep clears. Two sweep columns walk the floor on a schedule -
one up x=205, one up x=1030 - so the west and east walls are graves too. The one region no
sweep ever visits is the top-center strip against the north wall: x 560-660, y 60-140. On your
FIRST build order walk straight there (target [600, 95]) and settle flat against the north
wall. Touch NO crate this half: never push one, never lock one - seekers pry the nearest
locked crate the moment their heartbeat runs hot, so any crate you lock is a beacon planted on
your own spot; keep at least 200px between you and every locked crate. Be in your final spot,
still, before the build act ends. The moment the hunt begins set crawl true and freeze: a
still, crawling cog is silent and invisible, and footsteps are the only thing that betrays a
good spot. If a beam is reported, flee perpendicular to its bearing - leave the cone sideways,
never outrun it - break line of sight, then freeze again, and drift back to the strip once the
beam is gone.
As a seeker: on the first hunt order claim a third of the map by x (left is 0-410, middle
410-825, right 825-1235) and sweep it with intent "sweep", aim "sweep", targeting the far
corner of your third; do not follow your team-mates. Read the heartbeat every turn: cold or
cool means your third is empty and you should push deeper, warm means slow down and let the
beam do the work, hot or burning means stop advancing and sweep in place. On any lit hider
switch to "chase" with aim "track" and stay on it - half a second in the beam is a find. Only
pry a locked crate when the heartbeat is burning and nothing has been lit for two orders.
