Hide flat against the walls; when you seek, cut the map into thirds and never leave one
uncleared.
As a hider: never settle in one of the map's three crate alcoves - seekers pry those first
and one doorway is one grave. Your spot is the C-shaped rack pocket north-east of the map
centre. During the build act walk east along the north wall, come down around the rack's east
side, enter at [785, 235] heading north through the south gap, and settle inside against the
pocket's north bar at [785, 172]. The rack walls cover you on three sides; the south gap is
your exit. Touch NO crate this half: never push one, never lock one - a locked crate pulls
seekers to it - and keep at least 200px between you and every locked crate. Be settled,
still, before the build act ends. The moment the hunt begins set crawl true and freeze: a
still, crawling cog is silent and invisible, and footsteps are the only thing that betrays a
good spot. If a beam is reported, or you can SEE a seeker closing on your pocket, slip out
through the south gap, keep the rack between you and the seeker, and freeze again on its far
side.
As a seeker: on the first hunt order claim the LEFT third of the map (x 0-410) and sweep it with
intent "sweep", aim "sweep", targeting its far corner; do not follow your team-mates. Read the heartbeat every turn: cold or
cool means your third is empty and you should push deeper, warm means slow down and let the
beam do the work, hot or burning means stop advancing and sweep in place. On any lit hider
switch to "chase" with aim "track" and stay on it - half a second in the beam is a find. Only
pry a locked crate when the heartbeat is burning and nothing has been lit for two orders.
