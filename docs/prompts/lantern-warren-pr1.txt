Hide flat against the walls; when you seek, cut the map into thirds and never leave one
uncleared.
As a hider: never settle in one of the map's three crate alcoves - seekers pry those first
and one doorway is one grave. Your spot is the map's south-west corner, behind the short
wall stub that stands at x 120-136, y 470-570. On your FIRST build order walk STRAIGHT
south from your spawn, hugging the west wall the whole way, to [80, 575], and settle there
flat, with the wall stub between you and the open floor. It is open ground with two exits
(north along the west wall, east along the south wall), not an alcove. Touch NO crate this
half: never push one, never lock one - a locked crate pulls seekers to it - and keep at
least 200px between you and every locked crate. Be settled, still, before the build act
ends. The moment the hunt begins set crawl true and freeze: a still, crawling cog is silent
and invisible, and footsteps are the only thing that betrays a good spot. If a beam is
reported, or you can SEE a seeker closing on your corner, slide east along the south wall,
keep the stub between you and the seeker, and freeze again.
As a seeker: on the first hunt order claim the LEFT third of the map (x 0-410) and sweep it
with intent "sweep", aim "sweep"; do not follow your team-mates. Sweep the third as a fixed
serpentine of three tall passes: down its inner edge (x 410), up its middle lane (x 205) past
the nook mouths, down the west wall, then back again. Each order target the far end of your
current pass; when you reach it, or after at most two orders on one target, switch to the next
pass - never a third order on one target: a walking beam covers, a parked beam begs. After any
chase or pry, resume the pass you were on; never restart the serpentine. Heartbeat: cold or
cool, stride; warm, hot or burning, a hider is near this pass - rake the cover edges as you
walk, and KEEP WALKING. On any lit hider switch to "chase" with aim "track" and stay on it -
half a second in the beam is a find; a chase that lights nothing for two orders is a ghost -
drop it, resume your pass. A locked crate on your third is a hider's shield, and prying it open is part of YOUR
sweep: EVERY time the heartbeat reads hot or burning and nothing is lit while the crate at
the west nook mouth is still locked - not just the first time - your NEXT order is the pry:
walk STRAIGHT to that crate, stand at its edge and PRY until it pops - three still seconds
is the price of two finds - then resume the pass you were on. This pry outranks the sweep:
"rake the cover edges as you walk, and KEEP WALKING" never overrides it. If it has not
popped after two orders, leave it, finish the pass you are on, and answer the NEXT hot or
burning reading with the pry again - never two pry attempts without a pass between them.
