Hide flat against the walls; hunt with your ears, then your feet.
As a hider: never end a build act inside an alcove or nook - one doorway is one grave, and
alcoves are the first thing a sweep clears. During the build, push ONE loose crate into the
middle of a long sightline so it cuts the lane, lock it, and then take your spot: against an
outer wall, behind a pillar or rack, with your screen crate between you and the seeker pen and
two open directions to leave by. Do not stop beside your locked crate and never park on a nook
anchor. Be in your final spot, still, before the build act ends. The moment the hunt begins set
crawl true and freeze: a still, crawling cog behind cover is silent and invisible, and
footsteps are the only thing that betrays a good spot. If a beam is reported, flee
perpendicular to its bearing - leave the cone sideways, never outrun it - break line of sight
around your screen crate, then freeze again. Spend at most two locks; keep the third crate
loose so you can shove it behind you mid-hunt to close a lane, but only once the seekers
already know roughly where you are - a push is loud.
As a seeker: sounds and the heartbeat outrank any plan. Open by claiming the third of the map
(left, middle, right by x) nearest your pen exit that no teammate is already heading for, and
beeline to its centre with aim sweep. Re-read every turn: a push ring means someone is still
building a wall - go break in; a footstep ring means someone just moved - beeline to it.
Heartbeat is straight-line distance to the nearest unfound hider: cold or cool means your area
is empty, move on to the next unswept area; warm means sweep while advancing; hot or burning
means the hider is within one room - do NOT park and sweep in place: advance through the area,
swing the beam across every crate face and wall shadow, and walk the blind side of each crate
close enough to touch it, because your 60 px bubble and a 24 px touch both count. The instant
anything is lit, chase with aim track and never let go - half a second of light is a find. If
burning has lasted two turns with nothing lit, pry the nearest locked crate. Under 20 seconds
left with nothing lit, pry the nearest locked crate anyway: a broken crate is a hiding place
deleted from the map.
